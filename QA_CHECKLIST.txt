# MaChip (Microchip Attendance & Chip Payroll)
# COMPREHENSIVE END-TO-END SITUATIONAL QA CHECKLIST

This checklist provides a structured, situational end-to-end testing guide covering every module of MaChip. It follows realistic user lifecycles—from onboarding to hardware scans, requests, loan amortizations, batch payroll, payslip generation, and final settlements.

---

## 👥 System Personas for Testing
* **Admin Manager:** Full administrative access (User registration, Settings, Holidays, Reference Tables).
* **Admin Accountant:** Payroll, Loans, Statutory Deductions, 13th Month, Separation & Retirement.
* **Supervisor:** Attendance oversight, DTR reviews, Overtime & Leave request approvals.
* **Employee:** Clocking in/out, submitting requests, viewing personal DTR and released payslips.

## Module 2: RFID/Biometric Logging & Attendance Cycle

### 🎭 Scenario 2.1: Full Workday Punch Lifecycle
> **Context:** Testing live attendance punches, late arrivals, lunch breaks, and automated absence triggers.

- [ ] **Case A: On-Time Arrival (Standard Shift)**
  - Clock In: `08:25 AM` (Before `08:30 AM` start time)
  - Lunch Out / In: `12:00 PM` - `01:00 PM`
  - Clock Out: `05:31 PM`
  - Expected: Status is `Present` (ID: 1); Regular Hours = `8.00`, Tardiness = `0`.
- [ ] **Case B: Late Arrival with Penalty Calculation**
  - Clock In: `08:45 AM` (15 minutes after shift start)
  - Clock Out: `05:30 PM`
  - Expected: Status is `Late` (ID: 2); Regular Hours = `7.75`, Tardiness = `15 mins`.
- [ ] **Case C: Overtime Work Shift**
  - Employee has an approved 2.0-hour OT request.
  - Clock In: `08:30 AM` | Clock Out: `07:30 PM` (19:30)
  - Expected: Regular Hours = `8.00` + Approved OT Hours = `2.00` (Rate: 125%).
- [ ] **Case D: End-of-Day Automated Absence Marking**
  - Employee does not scan for a scheduled workday.
  - Trigger: System clock passes `5:30 PM` (17:30).
  - Expected: Daily shift-end task generates a DTR record with status **`Absent` (ID: 3)** and `absence_Hrs = 8.0`.

### 🛡️ Hardware & Logging Edge Cases
- [ ] **Rapid Scans (Debounce):** Scan the same RFID card 5 times within 2 seconds. (Expected: 1st logged, rest debounced).
- [ ] **Sunday Scans:** Scan card on a Sunday. Verify system logs event but excludes it from normal payroll workdays.

## Module 5: Payroll Processing & Multi-Tier Deduction Verification

### 🎭 Scenario 5.1: Generating & Auditing Batch Payroll
> **Context:** Admin Accountant processes semi-monthly payroll for the cutoff.

- [ ] **Step 1: Generate Batch Payroll**
  - Path: `Admin Dashboard` → `Payroll` → `Generate Payroll`.
  - Select Period: e.g. `2026-08-01` to `2026-08-15`.
  - Confirm batch creation.
- [ ] **Step 2: Confidentiality & CIA Triad Verification**
  - [ ] The generated batch status is **`Draft` (ID: 1)**.
  - [ ] Log in as Employee in another browser window: **The unreleased draft payslip MUST NOT appear in the employee portal**.
- [ ] **Step 3: Multi-Tier Calculation Audit (DetailsPayroll.jsx)**
  - Open Juan Dela Cruz’s payroll calculation card:
  
  $$\begin{aligned}
  \text{Workdays in Cutoff} &= 11 \text{ days} \\
  \text{Basic Pay} &= 11 \times ₱769.23 = \mathbf{₱8,461.53} \\
  \text{Worked Days} &= 10 \text{ days (1 day unexcused absence)} \\
  \text{Absence Deduction} &= 1 \times ₱769.23 = -\mathbf{₱769.23} \\
  \text{Tardiness Deduction} &= 15 \text{ mins} = -\mathbf{₱24.04} \\
  \text{Actual Gross Earnings} &= ₱8,461.53 - ₱769.23 - ₱24.04 = \mathbf{₱7,668.26}
  \end{aligned}$$

  - **Statutory Deductions (Semi-Monthly):**
    - SSS EE Share: $\approx ₱475.00$
    - PhilHealth EE Share: $\approx ₱200.00$
    - Pag-IBIG EE Share: $\approx ₱100.00$
    - Total Statutory: $₱775.00$
  - **Taxable Base & BIR Tax Evaluation:**
    - Taxable Base $= ₱7,668.26 - ₱775.00 = \mathbf{₱6,893.26}$
    - BIR Bracket 1 ($< ₱20,833$) $\rightarrow$ **Exempt (₱0.00 Tax)**.
  - **Post-Tax Loan Deductions:**
    - SSS Salary Loan: $\mathbf{₱500.00}$ *(Verify loans DO NOT reduce taxable income)*.
  - **Net Take-Home Pay:**
    - $\text{Net Pay} = ₱7,668.26 - (₱775.00 + ₱0.00 + ₱500.00) = \mathbf{₱6,393.26}$.
- [ ] **Step 4: Official Batch Release**
  - Admin reviews and clicks **`Release Payroll Batch`**.
  - Status updates to **`Released` (ID: 2)**.
  - Payslips become accessible to employees and email notifications are sent.
